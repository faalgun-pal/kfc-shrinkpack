About us

Established in 2019, KFC Shrinkpack Private Limited (also written KFC Shrink Pack) is a printing and packaging manufacturer serving food, beverage and consumer product businesses. The company is led by Mukesh Kumar, and our registered office is in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh.

Common questions

Instant answers come from our company information. For anything else, message us.
What is a shrink sleeve?

A printed plastic sleeve that slips over a bottle or container. When heated, it tightens into a skin around the pack.

Can you print my own design?

Yes. Add your artwork and size to the notes in your enquiry and we’ll confirm what’s possible.

How do I get a price?

Add products to your enquiry with the quantity you need, then send it by WhatsApp or email. We’ll reply with a quote.
